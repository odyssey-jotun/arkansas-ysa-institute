Questions about institute in central Arkansas

Class times, registration, credit and which ward is yours, for young adults in the Little Rock and North Little Rock stakes.

What is institute?

Institute of Religion is the weekly religion class that The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints offers young adults. Classes study the scriptures and the teachings of living prophets, and they meet in meetinghouses, campus buildings and homes. Many people search for it as LDS institute or myInstitute.

When and where does the Little Rock Stake institute class meet?

Tuesdays at 7:00 PM in a home in Benton. Sign up on myInstitute and we'll send you the address. The North Little Rock Stake schedule will appear on its stake tab when it is set.

Who can attend institute?

Anyone ages 18 to 35. Young single adults are the heart of the class, and married young adults are welcome. Where enough students sign up, classes split into ages 18 to 25 and 26 to 35. Adults over 35 can ask their ward or stake about other classes.

Is institute free?

Yes. Institute courses have no cost.

Do I have to be a member of the Church to attend?

No. Friends of other faiths are welcome. The myInstitute Quick Signup was built for them: it asks for a name, an email and a birthdate, with no Church account required, and the institute administrator approves the request.

How do I register on myInstitute?
  1. Go to myinstitute.churchofjesuschrist.org.
  2. Choose Find an Institute and search for Little Rock or North Little Rock, or choose Find Classes Near Me.
  3. On the program page, choose Find a Class.
  4. Pick the class and choose Register.
  5. Members choose Login & Register with a Church account. Friends choose Quick Signup.

Once you are registered, the class appears on your myInstitute dashboard.

Can I come without registering?

Yes. Visit a class first and register afterward. Registering near the start of a semester gives you the best chance of earning credit.

Can I take institute online?

Search myInstitute for online classes, which help when a work shift or a long drive keeps you from Tuesday night in Benton.

How do I earn institute credit?
  • Attend at least 75 percent of class meetings.
  • Finish the assigned reading: all of it for scripture courses, 75 percent for other courses.
  • Complete the course's learning experiences.

Credit counts toward institute graduation. Many institute courses also transfer as religion credit to Church schools such as BYU, BYU-Idaho, BYU-Hawaii and Ensign College, and each school sets its own transfer rules.

What is a YSA ward, and what ages count as young single adults?

Since July 2024, the Church counts unmarried members ages 18 to 35 as young single adults. Single adults 36 and older belong to the single adult program. A YSA ward or branch is a congregation for young single adults, organized where enough of them live. Your bishop or branch president can tell you which unit your address belongs to, and institute is open to you either way.

Which ward or branch do I attend?

Your home address decides it. The stake tabs on the home page list all 18 wards and branches in the two stakes. The meetinghouse locator on the Church's website shows exact boundaries.
