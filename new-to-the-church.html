New to the Church of Jesus Christ?

Plain answers to the questions friends of other faiths ask before their first visit.

Is LDS the same as Mormon? What should I call the Church?

Both are older nicknames for the same church. LDS is short for Latter-day Saints. In 2018, President Russell M. Nelson asked that the Church be called by its full name, The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Members are happy to be called Latter-day Saints or members of the Church of Jesus Christ. The Book of Mormon keeps its name.

What do Latter-day Saints believe?

Latter-day Saints worship God the Father and His Son, Jesus Christ. They believe the Church Christ organized in the New Testament was restored through the Prophet Joseph Smith in the 1800s, and that God still calls prophets today. They study the Bible and the Book of Mormon together, and they believe families can stay together after death through promises made in temples.

Do Latter-day Saints believe in Jesus? Are they Christians?

Yes. Jesus Christ's name is in the Church's name, and members pray, take the sacrament and seek forgiveness in His name. They believe He is the Son of God and the Savior of the world. Some Christian denominations do not classify the Church as Christian, chiefly over the nature of God and the Nicene Creed, which Latter-day Saints do not accept.

Do Latter-day Saints believe in the Trinity?

Latter-day Saints believe in God the Father, His Son Jesus Christ, and the Holy Ghost as three separate beings who are perfectly united in purpose. They call this the Godhead. The traditional Trinity of the Nicene Creed describes one being in three persons, which is where the two views part ways.

What is the Book of Mormon, and who wrote it?

The Book of Mormon is a volume of scripture that Latter-day Saints read alongside the Bible. It tells of ancient peoples in the Americas and of the resurrected Jesus Christ visiting them. Members believe ancient prophets wrote it, a prophet named Mormon abridged it, and Joseph Smith translated it by the power of God. It was first published in 1830.

Why don't Latter-day Saints drink coffee? Can they have caffeine?

The Church's health code, the Word of Wisdom (Doctrine and Covenants 89), asks members to avoid alcohol, tobacco, coffee, tea and harmful or addictive drugs. Church leaders have explained that the revelation's "hot drinks" means coffee and tea, so iced coffee is still coffee. Caffeine itself is not named, so a caffeinated soda is a personal choice.

Do Latter-day Saints drink alcohol?

No. Alcohol is part of the Word of Wisdom, so Latter-day Saints do not drink it, and institute activities are alcohol-free.

Do Latter-day Saints have multiple wives?

No. Some early members practiced plural marriage in the 1800s, and the Church ended the practice in 1890. Anyone who practices it today is excommunicated, and groups that still do are separate from the Church.

Do Latter-day Saints celebrate Christmas and Easter?

Yes, both. Christmas centers on the birth of Jesus Christ and Easter on His Resurrection. Wards hold Christmas programs and Easter Sunday services, and families keep the same holiday traditions as their neighbors.

What are temple garments?

Adult members who have made promises in the temple wear a simple white undergarment beneath their everyday clothes. It reminds them of those promises and of Jesus Christ. Members treat garments as sacred and keep them out of view.

Is there a temple in Arkansas?

Yes. The Bentonville Arkansas Temple, the first in the state, was dedicated on September 17, 2023. Anyone may attend Sunday services at a meetinghouse. Temples are reserved for ordinances such as eternal marriage, for members who hold a temple recommend.

Where does the Church of Jesus Christ meet near me in central Arkansas?

The two stakes have 18 wards and branches meeting in Little Rock, North Little Rock, Conway, Quitman, Morrilton, Russellville, Danville, Hot Springs, Pine Bluff, Malvern, Arkadelphia, Glenwood, Hope, Camden and Monticello. Sunday services are open to everyone. The stake tabs on the home page list each one.
