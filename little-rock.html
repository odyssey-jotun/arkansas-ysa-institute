A small class in Marc and Nikki's home

Marc and Nikki open their home on Millbrook Drive to the Little Rock Stake class every Tuesday night. A living room keeps the class small enough for real discussion, and first-timers walk in the front door like everyone else.

Earning institute credit

Credit counts toward institute graduation and, at many Church schools, toward religion requirements. To earn it:

  • Attend at least 75 percent of class meetings.
  • Finish the assigned reading: all of it for scripture courses, 75 percent for other courses.
  • Complete the course's learning experiences.

BYU, BYU-Idaho, BYU-Hawaii and Ensign College each set their own transfer rules.

Questions young adults ask first

When and where does the Little Rock Stake institute class meet?

Tuesdays at 7:00 PM at 2710 Millbrook Drive, Benton, AR 72015, a home in Benton. The North Little Rock Stake schedule will appear on its stake tab when it is set.

Who can attend institute?

Anyone ages 18 to 35. Young single adults are the heart of the class, and married young adults are welcome. Where enough students sign up, classes split into ages 18 to 25 and 26 to 35. Adults over 35 can ask their ward or stake about other classes.

Is institute free?

Yes. Institute courses have no cost.

Do I have to be a member of the Church to attend?

No. Friends of other faiths are welcome. The myInstitute Quick Signup was built for them: it asks for a name, an email and a birthdate, with no Church account required, and the institute administrator approves the request.

Can I come without registering?

Yes. Visit a class first and register afterward. Registering near the start of a semester gives you the best chance of earning credit.
